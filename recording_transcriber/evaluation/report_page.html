Transcript comparison

What is this? Several transcription models transcribed the same recording. At selected places you decide which version is right. Which version comes from which model is hidden on purpose.

It takes about an hour, also in several sittings. Your answers stay saved in this browser. When done, click Save answers and put the file answers.json into the Drive folder.

Part 1: words (about 30 minutes)

Listen to the clip and pick the version that matches what was said. Punctuation, capitalization and fillers ("äh") do not matter. The highlighted place is the one in question.

Part 2: speakers (about 15 minutes)

Optional, but it counts a lot. Who is speaking in this clip? Pick a name, or type one that is not listed. If you do not know the person, pick "Unknown person" with a number, and reuse the same number when the same voice comes up again.
